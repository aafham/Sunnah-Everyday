# Quran Content Policy

## Scope

Quran-related fields are not required for the initial product and no Quran text
or translation is bundled by default. If a future approved item cites Quran,
this policy applies alongside the source and review workflow.

## Requirements before display

- Record the canonical reference, edition/source, language, rights and display
  basis in the source register.
- Confirm that Arabic text, translation, transliteration and commentary have
  separate appropriate permissions where necessary.
- Preserve exact references and do not generate or editorially alter Quranic
  text or its translation.
- Obtain qualified human review for relevance and context.
- Render Arabic as selectable RTL text with a licensed font; do not put it in
  an image.

## Restrictions

Do not scrape or bulk copy databases, assume a public URL grants reproduction
rights, combine translations without provenance, or display a quote as proof of
a conclusion that has not passed the fiqh/context workflow.

## Failure handling

Missing rights, source, reference, context review, or display capability blocks
publication. Use an approved link-only treatment only where it does not copy
protected text and clearly identifies the external source.
