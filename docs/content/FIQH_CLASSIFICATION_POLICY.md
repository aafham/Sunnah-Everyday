# Fiqh and Classification Policy

## Separate concerns

Narration grade and practice classification are independent fields. A report
being graded does not by itself establish that a behaviour is recommended for
every user or circumstance.

## Supported classifications

`SUNNAH_RECOMMENDED`, `ADAB`, `AKHLAQ`, `OBLIGATORY`, `PERMISSIBLE`,
`PROPHETIC_HABIT`, `PROPHET_SPECIFIC`, `CONTEXT_SPECIFIC`, `DISPUTED`,
`HISTORICAL_INFORMATION`, `DUA`, and `DHIKR` are supported. Prophetic form is
stored separately as `QAWLIYYAH`, `FI_LIYYAH`, `TAQRIRIYYAH`, `MIXED`, or
`NOT_APPLICABLE`.

## Required review questions

The approved record must answer, where relevant: what the evidence is; the
classification; whether it is sought for practice; whether it may be a habit or
Prophet-specific; applicable conditions/audience/situation; known differences;
and common misunderstandings. The answers are reviewed content, not AI
inference.

## Writing rules

Use precise approved wording. Do not convert an optional matter into an
obligation, conceal a material difference, imply a reward not evidenced, or
give individual legal advice. A disputed/limited item must visibly retain that
scope and must not enter a generic Daily Feed without the applicable approvals.

## Gate

Fiqh/context approval is required for public content. The publisher cannot
substitute an editorial judgement for this review, and the publication gate
must reject an item missing required classification/context fields.
