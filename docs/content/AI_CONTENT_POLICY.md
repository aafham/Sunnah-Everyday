# AI Content Policy

## Permitted assistance

AI may assist with data structure, search over already-approved corpora,
editorial plain-language drafts, metadata completeness checks, tagging, and
technical consistency checks. It may not be an authority on religious content.

## Prohibited assistance

AI must not generate hadith, grade a narration, decide a legal classification,
issue fatwa, fill gaps with invented sources, translate/alter a source for
publication without required human review, create fake reviewers, or produce
public content from an unapproved prompt.

## Status and traceability

Any AI-influenced religious-language field is `DRAFT` or `NEEDS_REVIEW` until
a qualified human approves it through the normal workflow. The audit trail must
record the editor/reviewer decision and never state that AI is a reviewer or
source. AI output is not evidence.

## Product behaviour

Search may only retrieve approved content; it must not generate open-ended
religious answers. Empty states state that no approved content is available.
Logs must avoid private reflections, full protected source text, credentials,
and personal report data.
