# Reviewer Requirements

## Roles

The CMS supports `RESEARCHER`, `HADITH_REVIEWER`, `FIQH_REVIEWER`,
`LANGUAGE_EDITOR`, `TRANSLATOR`, `PUBLISHER`, `CONTENT_ADMIN`,
`TECHNICAL_ADMIN`, and `SUPER_ADMIN`. Roles grant workflow capabilities; they
do not establish a person's scholarly qualification.

## Reviewer record

A reviewer record stores verified name or approved public display name,
reviewer ID, role(s), qualifications, scope/specialism, evidence reference,
contact/identity data in restricted storage, active status, and review dates.
No person may be represented as qualified until the owner supplies and records
the actual information.

## Separation of duties

Each public item needs source/hadith review, fiqh/context review, language
review and final approval, with at least two distinct people overall. A
reviewer cannot self-approve their own authored version without the required
independent review. A publisher cannot alter a reviewer decision or bypass a
missing gate.

## Responsibilities

- **Researcher:** records sources/candidate metadata; cannot publish.
- **Hadith reviewer:** reviews recorded narration evidence and grade basis.
- **Fiqh reviewer:** reviews classification, context, scope and disagreement.
- **Language editor/translator:** checks faithful, understandable presentation.
- **Publisher:** schedules only fully approved immutable versions.
- **Technical/content admin:** manages systems/data within least privilege.

Reviewer qualifications and approvals are sensitive operational records. Public
display is limited to the approved profile fields; private details are not
available to public users or ordinary admins.
