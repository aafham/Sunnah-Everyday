# Content Review Checklist

Use this checklist for an immutable version before final approval. A checked
box represents a human reviewer decision backed by the database/audit record,
not a self-attestation.

## Source and rights

- [ ] Source owner, edition, locator, language and access date are recorded.
- [ ] Display mode matches `GRANTED`, `PUBLIC_LICENSE`, or `LINK_ONLY` scope.
- [ ] No protected text is copied without the required permission.
- [ ] Every displayed evidence/source link resolves to the recorded locator.

## Evidence and context

- [ ] Evidence type, reference, narrator/collection where applicable, and
      rights status are complete.
- [ ] Grade and assessor are recorded from an authorised review/source.
- [ ] Daily Feed eligibility is `SAHIH` or `HASAN`; prohibited grades are not
      scheduled.
- [ ] Classification and prophetic form are separate and approved.
- [ ] Conditions, scope, disagreement, misconceptions and relevant caveats are
      retained rather than editorially removed.

## Language and presentation

- [ ] BM and English fields are complete or intentionally unavailable with an
      approved rationale.
- [ ] No invented quotation, reward claim, judgement, shame, or fatwa appears.
- [ ] Arabic is text with correct RTL/font treatment and rights basis.
- [ ] Source, grade, reviewer/date, version and correction state are visible as
      appropriate to public users.

## Workflow and release

- [ ] Source/hadith, fiqh/context, language and final decisions are complete.
- [ ] At least two distinct qualified people reviewed the public item.
- [ ] Version is immutable, checksummed and fully audited.
- [ ] No placeholder/demo flag, missing rights, withdrawn state, or failed gate
      remains.
