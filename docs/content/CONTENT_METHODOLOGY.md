# Content Methodology

## Purpose and scope

This methodology governs all public religious content. Sunnah Everyday is a
general educational product, not a fatwa service. It prioritises accuracy,
traceability, transparent uncertainty, privacy, and a calm learning experience.
It does not claim affiliation with any religious institution without written
authority.

## Content layers

1. **Primary text:** a permitted primary source identified by the review panel.
2. **Narration assessment:** grade, grader/institution, edition/locator, and
   verification date.
3. **Authoritative explanation:** an approved institution or scholarly source.
4. **Editorial summary:** plain-language explanation that preserves scope,
   conditions, disagreement and uncertainty.

An editorial summary may never create a ruling, promise a reward, remove a
material qualification, or make a recommendation stronger than the review
record supports.

## Required metadata

Every item/version must have a stable ID, content type, category, classification,
prophetic form, intended situation/audience, bilingual fields where applicable,
evidence links, rights status, source locator, reviewer records, workflow state,
version number, timestamps, and audit trail. Items with a scholarly difference,
medical caveat, Prophet-specific scope, or limited audience must expose the
relevant flag and approved explanatory note.

## Public publication standard

Public content is eligible only when all are true:

- It is an immutable version with a checksum and audit history.
- Source rights allow the actual display mode (link-only or licensed text).
- Evidence and any narration grade are attributable to a recorded source.
- Hadith/source, fiqh/context, language, and final approvals are recorded.
- At least two separate qualified human reviewers participated.
- The publication gate accepts the workflow state and all required metadata.

Daily Feed is a stricter subset: only `SAHIH` or `HASAN` narration grades may
be scheduled; source and reviewer requirements still apply.

## Transparency and corrections

Show source links, grade and assessor, classification, context, review date,
reviewer public identity where approved, and version/correction history. A
reported concern can suspend a daily item before resolution. Corrections create
a new version; withdrawals remove content from cache/widget availability while
retaining the audit record.

## Prohibited practices

Never use AI-generated religious content as public data; manufacture citations;
present placeholder data as real; use viral claims as a source; use reward,
shame or comparative worship mechanics; or hide relevant uncertainty.
